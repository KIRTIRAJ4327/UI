# Principal UX/UI designer review: Unified Product Delivery Platform (frozen copy, localhost:4174)

Method: Playwright at 1440x900 and 1280x800 (DPR 1) on all 20 routes as alex and dana, plus sam for Release. I measured computed type, colour and focus on each page, did a scripted keyboard-only approval and a dialog test, and read the source in /home/user/UI/app/src/v3 (React and Tailwind). Rules come from the ui-ux-pro-max skill (`--design-system --density 8`, `--domain ux`, `--domain chart`, `--stack shadcn`, plus references/pro-rules.md). I did not change any project files.

## 1. Verdict
The product reads calm and trustworthy. Its colours are restrained and its microcopy is unusually plain and humane. The approval surfaces (Approve, Release confirmation) are close to Stripe and Linear quality. Underneath, it is a hand-tuned prototype rather than a system: 21 arbitrary font sizes, three heading sizes, hand-rolled dialogs without focus traps, two segmented-control styles, and decorative motion borrowed from marketing sites. For power users it is under-dense and keyboard-thin: no shortcuts beyond Ctrl+K, a command palette that only navigates, no bulk actions, no loading or skeleton states, and focus is lost after every route change and every overlay. Fixing the token layer and the keyboard and focus model would raise it from "polished demo" to "premium tool" faster than any visual restyle.

## 3. Interaction and craft improvements (ranked)

1. **Focus management everywhere (S).** Move focus to the `<h1>` (tabindex -1) on every route change. Return focus to the trigger when the palette or notifications close: today it falls to BODY in both. After Approve, focus the toast's Undo or the run's new status. *ux "Keyboard Navigation", "Focus States".*
2. **Use shadcn primitives for every overlay (M).** Use AlertDialog for Release and Roles, Command for the palette, Popover for notifications, and Sheet for "How the agent did this". This gives focus trap, inert background, Esc anywhere, and scroll lock.
3. **Fix the citation links (S).** `Cite` uses `href="#src"`, which the hash router treats as a route. Clicking a footnote on Approve leads to "This page does not exist" (cite-click.png). Use a button that scrolls to `#sources` with `scrollIntoView` and a target highlight, and give each one `aria-label="Source 1: ADR-0151"` (they are currently announced as "1", "2", "3").
4. **Make the command palette a real command layer (M).** Today "approve" and "release" return nothing, and Tab does nothing. Add an Actions group (Approve WI-4821, Request changes, New request, Switch person, Toggle theme, Copy link), a Recent group, per-item shortcut hints, scoped search (`>` actions, `#` runs, `@` people), and the nav icons. Linear and Vercel set this bar.
5. **Keyboard shortcuts (M).** `?` opens a shortcut sheet, `g h` / `g a` / `g r` go to pages, `j` / `k` move between list rows, `Enter` opens, `a` approves and `r` requests changes on a gate (with a confirmation for irreversible actions), and `Cmd+Enter` submits. Today the only shortcuts are Ctrl+K, `/` and Ctrl+Enter in comments.
6. **Make Home a dense inbox (M).** Three identical filled blue "Review" buttons compete with each other. Make the whole row the target, put one filled primary on the focused or hovered row, and show a ghost button elsewhere. Each card costs 3 tab stops (ID link, title link, Review), so reaching the third item takes 25 Tabs. Use one link per row plus roving tabindex. About 260px of empty space sits under the list at 1440x900; use it for a "Due soon / at risk" strip. *ux "Bulk Actions", density 8/10 from the design-system query.*
7. **Tables: bulk select, whole-row click, sticky header, density toggle (M).** Runs and Approvals rows have `cursor:auto` and no checkboxes. Add multi-select with a contextual action bar (Reassign, Nudge approver, Export) and a Comfortable or Compact toggle (rows are currently 67px; compact should be about 40px). Governance "All work items" repeats "Manage squad" 8 times and wraps titles to 3 lines in a two-thirds-width column. Make it full width, or link it to Runs with a filter. *ux "Bulk Actions: allow multi-select and bulk edit".*
8. **Loading, skeleton and optimistic states (M).** There are no loading or skeleton states in v3 (0 matches). Approve and Release switch instantly. Add a 150 to 250ms pressed or pending state on decision buttons, an optimistic stage advance with a reconciling toast, skeleton rows for tables, and an `aria-busy` region. *ux "Loading Indicators: preserve layout, focus and accessible busy status"; "Submit Feedback: show loading then success/error".*
9. **Toasts (S).** The approval toast followed the user to another page and another person (sam-release.png: "Design approved…" over Sam's Release page), which contradicts the fixed item "toasts close when you leave the page". It also overlaps a card edge. Anchor toasts bottom-right in a single portal stack, and make Undo reachable by shortcut (`z`) because it is about 40 Tabs away. *ux "Toast Notifications".*
10. **Data visualisation on Governance and Home (M).**
    - "Where work waits": colours bars red, amber and green with no legend, target or units ("9.5" bare). Add a 2h target rule line, units ("9.5 h"), sorted bars, and a single hue with the over-target bars in red plus an icon.
    - "People load": add a capacity marker.
    - KPI tiles on both pages: no delta, trend or target. Add a sparkline and a "▲ 2 vs last week" delta when there are 4 or more points, otherwise the stat card alone.
    *chart "Compare Categories: bar, sort descending"; "Trend: line; fewer than 4 points use stat card"; ux "Color Only".*
11. **Motion diet (S).** Home uses SplitText (an h1 that fades in word by word on every visit), SpotlightCard (a cursor-following glow, which made the third "Waiting" row look selected in my screenshot) and AnimatedList. These are marketing effects in a work tool. Keep motion for state change only: a stage advance (200ms), a toast entering (180ms), a drawer (240ms ease-out). Define `--dur-1/2/3` and `--ease-out` tokens. Reduced-motion rules exist (2), which is good. *ux "Duration Timing: shared motion tokens"; Animation: "Motion conveys meaning".*
12. **Make the code review feel credible (L).** It shows one expanded file, no line numbers, no inline comments, and no split view. Add a file tree, line numbers, per-line comment, "Viewed" checkboxes per file, and `n` / `p` to move between files (as on GitHub).
13. **Warning semantics (S).** The run page's "Approve the solution design" banner uses an amber warning triangle for a routine decision. Use an inbox or gavel icon in blue; keep the triangle for "The gap you are accepting".
14. **Inline edit (M).** Squads "Edit" and People roles should edit in a popover or combobox in the cell, not navigate away. Release notes should switch to a "View" and "Edit" toggle rather than an always-open textarea.

## 5. Dark-mode guidance for this palette
Source already has a `.v3[data-mode="dark"]` token block. Measured against it:

- **Surfaces are too close.** bg `#0d111d`, surface `#151a2a` and sidebar `#090c16` measure 1.09:1 (surface to bg) and 1.04:1 (sidebar to bg). Cards will disappear and the navy sidebar merges into the canvas. Use an elevation ladder made by lightness, not shadow: bg `#0b0f1a`, surface `#141a2b`, raised `#1b2236`, overlay `#232b42`, with a 1px `--v-line` of `#2a3350` on cards. Separate the sidebar by giving it the surface tone plus a right border, rather than making it darker than the canvas.
- **Keep the sage DNA.** The light theme's identity is sage `#eef1ed` against navy `#101429`; the dark ramp is pure blue-navy and loses it. Tint neutrals slightly green-grey (for example ink-2 `#b7c0bb`, ink-3 `#8e9a94`, line `#2a332f`) or keep a desaturated sage `#9fb3a6` for dividers and focus halos, so both modes feel like the same brand.
- **Blue `#2469bc`** is 3.31:1 on navy, so it is unusable for text in dark mode. Keep `#7aa7ff` for links and text (6.1:1 on blue-soft). The filled primary `#2f74d6` with white text is 4.58:1, which passes AA with little margin. Do not lighten it further, or move to `#3b7fe0` with near-black text.
- **Status colours**: amber `#f2b54b` and red `#ff8077` work as text. Stepper bars need their own tokens (`--v-bar-wait`, `--v-bar-done`, `--v-bar-empty #2a3350`) because `#e0a000` is hard-coded. Soft fills (`*-soft`) should be 12 to 16% alpha of the hue over the surface, not opaque hexes, so they sit correctly on both surface and raised.
- **Placeholder** `#737d93` on sunken is 3.87:1. Raise it to about `#8a94a9`.
- **Before shipping**:
  - Replace the hard-coded white surfaces (about 59 `bg-white` and 29 `text-white` in the frozen copy).
  - Swap the shadow tokens for borders.
  - Give avatar hues a dark variant (the AI avatar `#101429` disappears on a navy canvas; give it a 1px ring).
  - Scale the SpotlightCard glow to 0.14 or remove it.
  - Check code-diff green `#e6f4e8` → `#12291d`.
  - Set `color-scheme: dark` so native scrollbars and date pickers follow; it is already present.
  
  *pro-rules "Checked dark mode contrast independently; never assume light-mode values carry over"; "Primary and secondary text ≥4.5:1 in both modes".*
